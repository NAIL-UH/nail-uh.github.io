Human–AI Collaboration & Multi-Agent Autonomy

We investigate intelligent collaboration between humans and autonomous agents, as well as coordination among interconnected robotic systems. Our research focuses on human-autonomy teaming, trust-aware decision-making, adaptive task allocation, cooperative perception, and distributed control to enable effective and resilient multi-agent operations.

  • Human trust in automation and how automation communicates its reliability
  • Adaptive task allocation for human-machine teams, from manufacturing to m:N operations of uncrewed aircraft
  • Perception-aware distributed formation control for teams of drones and ground robots
  • Resilient vehicle platoons and networked control over unreliable wireless links
