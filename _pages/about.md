The NAIL Lab advances safe, intelligent, and collaborative autonomous systems that operate reliably in dynamic, uncertain, and resource-constrained environments. Bridging **machine learning**, **control theory**, **robotics**, and **embedded AI**, we design learning-based controllers and planners with formal safety guarantees, help people and teams of robots and vehicles work together through trust-aware task allocation and distributed control, bring reliable onboard planning to spacecraft and planetary robots, and build efficient perception models that run in real time on embedded hardware. We validate our methods on drones, ground robots, quadrupeds, and microcontrollers.
