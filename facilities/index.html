Facilities

The NAIL Lab is located in Engineering Building 4 (SAB2), Room 111, on the University of Houston Sugar Land campus. A heterogeneous robot fleet, a motion-capture testing arena, a multi-GPU training server, and a wide range of edge-AI hardware let us take our methods from simulation to real experiments, from learning-based control and multi-robot coordination to efficient perception on microcontrollers.

  • 14 aerial, ground, and legged robots
  • 4 NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell GPUs
  • 22 edge-AI boards and microcontrollers
  • 24 × 24 × 8 ft motion-capture volume

Testing arena and lab space

Our indoor testing arena is covered by an OptiTrack motion-capture system that tracks robots with sub-millimeter precision. The enclosed space lets us safely test formation control, swarm behaviors, multi-robot collaboration, and human-robot interaction.

Aerial robots

Drones for multi-drone coordination, swarm, and edge-AI experiments.

  • 3 units

    ModalAI Seeker SLAM drone

    Drones with onboard visual SLAM, navigation, and mapping, used for multi-drone coordination and swarm experiments.

  • ModalAI Sentinel drone

    VOXL 2 development drone with 5G connectivity and onboard AI processing for edge-AI and high-bandwidth communication research.

  • 4 units

    Crazyflie 2.1 Brushless AI bundle

    Nano quadcopters with an AI-deck for onboard neural network inference, used for swarm and edge-AI research.

  • Self-assembled AI drone

    Drone designed and built in the lab for specialized experiments and rapid prototyping.

Ground and legged robots

Wheeled and legged robots for formation control, multi-agent coordination, perception, and locomotion research.

  • 3 units

    ROSbot 2.0 Pro

    Ground robots with a full sensor suite for formation control and multi-agent coordination experiments.

  • Clearpath Jackal J100

    Ground robot with a Velodyne VLP-16 3D lidar and an Intel RealSense D435 camera for perception and navigation research.

  • Unitree Go2 quadruped

    Legged robot for research on dynamic locomotion, balance control, and navigation over complex terrain.

Computing and edge AI

A GPU server for training large models, and embedded boards for running them on board, in real time and on a power budget.

  • 4 GPUs

    Custom AI server

    In-house server for large-scale training and inference with four NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell Max-Q GPUs, an AMD Ryzen Threadripper 9975WX (32 cores), 192 GB of DDR5-6400 memory, and a 4 TB NVMe SSD.

  • 3 units

    NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin 64 GB

    Developer kits with up to 275 TOPS of AI performance for onboard autonomy in robots and drones.

  • NVIDIA Jetson Xavier NX

    Compact module with 21 TOPS of accelerated computing for edge AI.

  • 5 units

    NVIDIA Jetson Nano kits

    Four Jetson Orin Nano kits and one original Jetson Nano for prototyping and deploying AI at the edge.

  • 10 units

    MAX78000 and MAX78002 microcontrollers

    Ultra-low-power microcontrollers with a hardware CNN accelerator, used in our neural architecture search work. Six MAX78000 and two MAX78002 evaluation kits, and two MAX78000 Feather boards.

  • 3 units

    STM32F746NG Discovery kits

    Arm Cortex-M7 boards with a touchscreen and Ethernet for embedded AI and systems development.

Sensors and fabrication

Sensors for perception experiments, beyond the motion-capture system above, and a 3D printer for building custom parts.

  • 2 units

    Intel RealSense D435i

    RGB-D cameras with a built-in IMU for depth sensing, visual odometry, and 3D perception.

  • Livox Mid-360 lidar

    360° lidar that produces dense point clouds for mapping and navigation.

  • Hokuyo UST-10LX

    Scanning laser rangefinder for precise distance measurement in navigation and mapping.

  • Prusa MK4 3D printer

    Enclosed 3D printer for rapid prototyping and custom robot parts.

Equipment in action

  • Autonomous drone landing on a moving ground robot

    A drone lands on a moving ground vehicle, showing precise real-time control and communication between robots.

  • Seeker drone mapping, motion planning, and object detection

    The ModalAI Seeker drone builds a map with SLAM, plans its motion autonomously, and detects objects in real time.

  • Fully autonomous Jackal robot

    The Jackal robot runs SLAM, avoids moving obstacles, and detects objects indoors and outdoors.
