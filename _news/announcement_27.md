:trophy: Congratulations to **Richie Suganda** on receiving three competitive travel awards: the **CoRL 2026 Travel Award**, the **IROS 2026 Student and Developing Countries (SDC) Travel Award**, and the **UH Cullen Fellowship Travel Grant**. The CoRL and IROS awards support his presentations of our papers [Adaptive Risk-Certified Event-Triggered Replanning for Dynamic Navigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09302) at _CoRL 2026_ and [Formation-Aware Adaptive Conformalized Perception for Safe Leader-Follower Multi-Robot Systems](https://nail-uh.github.io/iros2026.github.io/) at _IROS 2026_.
